## 5. Limits of this evidence

* The closure bars are met for the audit cases (free floe, no gravity, no drag): criteria (e), (f) of the node.
* Inside the main solver the *undamped* run closes at the level the explicit-Euler scheme allows; the residual is first order in `dt` and is reported, not tuned away.
* The production damping run does **not** close until the tool/bow dashpot dissipation is integrated by the caller: `dem3d` does not accumulate that channel. The audit therefore reports an unaccounted channel instead of hiding it, and the `accounted` case proves the attribution (see `integration_demo.json`).
* Rotational kinetic energy is zero in the `dem3d` integration because that solver has translational degrees of freedom only; the rotational ledger is exercised by the audit module itself.
