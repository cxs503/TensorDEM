## 1. Problem, and what exactly is being measured

Random close packing (RCP) of equal spheres is the meta-stable limit of a
quasi-static packing; the literature value is **φ_RCP = 0.6366** (Scott 1960,
*Nature* 188:908; Bernal & Mason 1960, *Nature* 188:910). The anchor's
acceptance is *a porosity of a settled/poured deposit*, i.e. a **measured**
quantity.

Two prior deliverables left the anchor open:

| prior work | claim | defect found by |
|---|---|---|
| `bedcoupling.packing.make_random_close_packing` (m16179) | φ = 0.6400, 0.53 % from 0.6366, all gates pass | `docs/AUDIT_m16308_CASE-DEM-RCP-PACKING-FRACTION.md`: the routine rescales the cell to the target porosity, so the reported φ is `1 - ε` to 1e-16 — a restatement of the input (tautology control reproduced below) |
| `friction_surface.packing` L-S inflation (P152W1-N04) | φ = 0.6160 ± 0.0018, "3.75 % < 5 % → PASS" | passed against a self-declared 5 % tolerance, not the 3 % rule; the deficit was never explained |

The L-S inflation route *is* a measurement: the reported φ is the density at
which the quench can no longer remove overlaps, which is not an input. But the
protocol has two knobs that must influence the answer by construction — the
inflation rate and the relaxation budget per step — and the P152W1 deliverable
used exactly **one** setting of each. This document measures both, plus finite
size and the jamming criterion, and extrapolates the rate to zero.

## 2. Method

* Cell: periodic cube, N monodisperse spheres, `phi_start = 0.30`, uniform random
  initial positions, seed = 1/2.
* Kinematics: identical-radius inflation, `ρ` per step → `d ln φ/d step = 3 ln(1+ρ)`;
  after each inflation step the overlap field is relaxed by a capped
  Jacobi steepest-descent quench (`relax_steps` iterations, `under_relax = 0.5`).
* Jamming: declared when the mean overlap / diameter exceeds `overlap_stop`; φ_J is
  linearly interpolated to the criterion between the last two inflation steps, so
  the number is a hard-sphere limit of the measured inflation curve, not the last
  visited density.
* Scan matrix (pre-registered in `run_scan.py:configs()`, 17 configurations):
  rate ρ ∈ {2e-4, 1e-4, 5e-5, 2.5e-5} × seeds {1,2}; relax_steps ∈ {30, 60, 120, 240};
  N ∈ {500, 1000, 2000, 4000, 8000}; overlap_stop ∈ {1e-4, 1e-3, 1e-2}.
* Comparison statements are made against **0.6366 with the 3 % rule**; the anchor
  band 0.62–0.66 is reported separately.
* Code: `benchmarks/rcp_ls_extrapolation/{run_scan,analyse,build_doc}.py`;
  tests `tests/test_friction_surface_packing_rate.py`.

Two things were also **fixed in the library** (defects, not scan work):

1. `PackingCase.mu` was a phantom knob: it was echoed into the result dict while
   the contact law was frictionless normal-only. A quench has no tangential
   displacement history, so a Coulomb cone cannot be evaluated there at all;
   `mu != 0` now raises `NotImplementedError` instead of silently doing nothing.
2. The module docstring described overdamped Langevin dynamics
   (`v ← (F - k v)/γ`) that the code never implemented; it now describes the
   capped-descent quench actually run, and says that the rate/budget dependence
   is measured rather than assumed away.

### 3.5 Best estimate and the verdict

Inside the group N = 1000, ρ = 0.0001 (one protocol, so no confound):
the shallow budget gives φ_J = 0.5985 (relax_steps = 30), the deepest measured gives φ_J = 0.6085 (relax_steps = 60), i.e. a budget correction of +0.0101.

## 5. Notes, limitations, scope

1. **The 5 %-tolerance claim is retired.** P152W1-N04's "PASS" used a 5 % band it
   declared itself. Under the 3 % rule the single-point value 0.6160 fails, and the
   deficit is systematic (it decreases monotonically as the rate is lowered), not noise.
2. **The quench is not L-S dynamics.** The original lubrication-based inflation uses
   EDMD/overdamped dynamics; here it is a capped Jacobi descent. That is why a rate
   and a budget dependence exist at all, and both are reported instead of hidden.
3. **Additivity of the two corrections is an assumption.** The expensive corner
   (slowest rate × largest budget) was not run; it would tighten, not change, the
   conclusion sign.
4. **Friction is out of scope for this protocol, and now loud about it.** The
   anchor text asks for “no friction / low friction” tiers; a quench cannot carry a
   tangential history, so the frictional tier belongs to a time-stepping engine
   (`dem3d`/`SphereGas`) and is explicitly *not* claimed here.
5. **The anchor's 10 000-sphere wording** is covered to N = 8000 with the finite-size
   trend extrapolated; a literal N = 10 000 run is a further single configuration,
   not a new capability.
6. **Scope**: monodisperse spheres, periodic cell, no gravity, no walls. A poured
   deposit (gravity, wall friction, free surface) is a different measurement and is
   not what 0.6366 refers to.

## 6. Error judgement (per quantity, is it < 3 %?)

| quantity | value | reference | error | < 3 %? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| φ_J at the old single point (ρ = 0.0002) | 0.5981 | 0.6366 | -6.04% | **no** |
| rate→0 extrapolation (all rates) | 0.6190 | 0.6366 | -2.77% | yes |
| **best estimate** (slowest rate + budget correction) | 0.6085 | 0.6366 | +4.41% | **no** |
| tautological geometric route (m16179) | 0.6400 | 0.6366 | 0.53% | not applicable — the number is `1 - ε` to 1e-16 (input restatement) |

**Explicit answer**: **no** — the completed subset does *not* close the 3 %
rule (best estimate 0.6085, +4.41 % vs 0.6366). What the measurements do
establish is the *direction and size* of each protocol bias:
lowering the inflation rate by 2× gains +1.04 percentage points of φ_J, doubling the relaxation budget gains +1.01 pp, and the jamming **criterion** moves φ_J by 8.2 pp across 1e-4…1e-2 — the largest single knob.
A 3 % adjudication is therefore only meaningful once the criterion is fixed
(the anchor's own wording is a porosity, not an overlap threshold), which is
the recommendation handed to the next round. The earlier deliverable's
"PASS at 5 %" is withdrawn: under the 3 % rule its single point fails, and it
was measured with the two strongest biases at their most biased setting.
No friction-dependent claim is made (see §5.4).
