## ① Case

Frozen primary case of the campaign, unchanged: `d = 3 mm`, `ρ = 2500 kg/m³`,
`D/d = 5`, silo radius 24 mm, fill height 36 mm, `μ = 0.50`, `μ_roll = 0.05`,
`e = 0.30`, `k_n = 5e3 N/m`, wall friction 0 (smooth plate), 0.06 s settle,
0.12 s discard, 8 × 0.05 s windows, `W` = mean of the last 4 windows, seed 1.

## ② Question and method

m23184 found two archived records of this case with `W` = 0.0307498 and
0.0033577 kg/s (9.2 ×) and could not separate *code state* from *bistability*.
Three independent checks are run here:

1. **arithmetic** — recompute the two spellings of “20 steps per contact
   period”, `0.05·T_c` (`Material.dt`) and `T_c/20` (`run_level.py`), and
   compare them bit-for-bit with the `dt` fields stored in the archives;
2. **dynamical** — build the bed **once**, deep-copy it, shift `dt` by
   ±1 representable step and step both copies in lockstep, recording the
   trajectory separation and the cumulative particle count (paired probe,
   `probe_divergence.py`); a same-`dt` copy pair is the determinism control;
3. **full protocol** — re-run the frozen case at each `dt` spelling
   (`run_ulp.py`, shards A/B) to reproduce the archived numbers.

## ⑤ Caveats

* The paired probes simulate only 0.04–0.12 s after the outlet opens (a single
  0.58 s record costs ≈ 250 s of wall clock and the compute gate is saturated).
  They establish the *mechanism* and its time scale; they do not measure a
  branch probability.
* λ is a finite-time estimate (log-linear fit over 1e-6 ≤ median |Δx|/d ≤ 1e-1),
  not a Lyapunov exponent of the infinite-time flow.
* The archive records are read-only inputs from other worktrees and are quoted
  with their path and commit.
* Scope: one frozen configuration (`D/d = 5`, monodisperse, smooth flat plate).
  Nothing here transfers to conical hoppers, wall friction or polydisperse beds.
