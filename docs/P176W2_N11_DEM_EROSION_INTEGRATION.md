### honest FAIL (not gated, not overwritten)

Absolute agreement with DNV at the pipe operating corner is 517x (threshold 15%). the pipe's wall impacts are slow (0.8-1.8 m/s) and grazing (4-9 deg), i.e. far outside both laws' calibration windows: DNV's ductile K, n are high-speed sand-service values and its angle function F = sin(alpha)^n (the form implemented in tensordem.erosion.models; the full RP-O501 ductile F has a peak near 45 deg, so this repo's form is a simplification) collapses at grazing angles, while the Oka plastic form keeps a cutting-dominated lobe near 20 deg.  The decomposed speed factor and the shape factor in replay_decomposition show which part of the gap is extrapolation and which is angle shape.  This is a model-class divergence, not an implementation error; the campaign criteria C1-C7 are adjudicated at matched conditions in benchmarks/erosion_dem_verification.py and pass there.
