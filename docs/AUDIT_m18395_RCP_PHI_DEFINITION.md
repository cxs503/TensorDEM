# AUDIT — RCP deposition solid fraction: is the measurement operator harmless?

Anchor: `CASE-DEM-RCP-PACKING-FRACTION` (feature_ledger) · repo TensorDEM · every number below is generated by `benchmarks/case_rcp_deposition/phi_definition_audit.py` from the stored settled configurations `runs/*.npz` (no new simulation, no hand-copied value).

## 1 Why this audit

The acceptance criterion is a *band* on the solid fraction of a poured packing, and the merged document reports one estimator: the mean of a cloud-in-cell profile over `[2.5 d, H - 2.5 d]`. The physics systematics of this case are audited elsewhere (stiffness ladder `G5/G6/G12/G13`, timestep `G14`, finite size `G7`, steady state and isostaticity, crystal order). The remaining degree of freedom is the read-out: the control volume, the quadrature and the overlap handling are *analyst choices*. If `phi` moves by O(1 %) between defensible choices, a PASS describes the read-out rather than the packing.

## 8 Notes and honest limits

* This audit re-analyses stored configurations only. It adds no new physics and cannot repair the physics systematics already on record: `G5/G6` stiffness convergence FAIL, `G9` residual overlap FAIL (`max overlap/d` = 0.0115 vs a 0.01 gate), `G13` frictional hard-sphere limit −3.07 %. It bounds how much of the reported number is measurement choice.
* `D8`/`D9` are volume-balance estimators: they divide raw sphere volume by a column height, so they carry free-surface roughness and contact double counting. They are reported to show *why* the band read-out is the right one, not as competing answers.
* The Monte-Carlo estimator is unbiased for the union volume, but its standard error is only ~1e-4 at the point counts used; differences below that are not resolved here, and the estimator is not a substitute for a larger contact-accurate volume integration.
* Polydispersity, a resolved container wall and long-time creep remain out of scope, as already recorded in `docs/CASE_RCP_DEPOSITION.md`.
* The audit depends on the stored `runs/*.npz` being the configurations that produced the merged metrics; `cic_reproduction_error` in §3 verifies exactly that (it is at machine precision for every run).

## 9 Verdict

**Quadrature and overlap treatment are harmless.** All seven estimators land inside the acceptance band on every production run, the within-run spread is 0.0041 against a 0.04-wide band (i.e. 10 % of it), the frictionless tier stays within 3 % of the reference under every estimator, and the seed-mean tier ordering survives all seven. The residual deviation from 0.6366 is therefore physics (soft-sphere stiffness, monodispersity and the frictional tier), not read-out.

**The control volume is NOT a free choice** — this is the one material analyst degree of freedom. Dropping the floor and free-surface layers is what puts the number in the band: the whole-bed read-out (`margin = 0`) gives phi = 0.59600..0.61591, i.e. below the 0.62 lower bound on 6 of 6 production runs, and the `0.5 d` margin is still below it on 3 runs. From `1 d` upward the band is control-volume invariant (phi = 0.62384..0.64260 at `1 d`, the merged `2.5 d` column and beyond). **The acceptance criterion must therefore be read as `phi_bulk` on the bulk band with >= 2.5 d dropped at floor and free surface, not as `稳态孔隙率` of the whole bed** — under the whole-bed reading the tier means are 0.61546 (mu = 0, 0.7 % below the 0.62 lower bound) and 0.59766 (mu = 0.1, 3.6 % below the 0.62 lower bound).

## 10 Recommendation for the anchor's acceptance text

The feature-ledger acceptance for `CASE-DEM-RCP-PACKING-FRACTION` reads `落下堆积体稳态孔隙率 (10000 球, 无摩擦/低摩擦两档)` with the band `0.62-0.66`. This audit shows that the number is well defined only once the control volume is pinned, because the floor and free-surface layers carry a deficit that is larger than the whole quadrature spread (§4). Suggested wording, fully supported by the numbers here:

> 指标 = 体相带内的体积分数 `phi_bulk`：CIC 剖面（bin = 0.5 d）在 `[2.5 d, H - 2.5 d]` 上的均值，其中 `H` 为自由表面高度 (`max z + r`)，底板层与自由表面层各丢弃 `2.5 d`；接受带 `0.62 <= phi_bulk <= 0.66`；参考值 `0.6366`（Scott 1960；Bernal & Mason 1960），容差 3 %；N = 10000，无摩擦与低摩擦（mu = 0.1）两档，每档 >= 3 个独立种子。

With that wording every gate in §5 except `A9` holds, and `A9` is the gate that forces exactly this wording.
